Your pregnancy, week by week.

A calm, private pregnancy companion for iPhone, made in Greek for Greece: what the baby is "building" each week, which test comes next and which paperwork is due.

Get it on the App Store · Free

Boumpouki (Μπουμπούκι, "little bud") is written in Greek and follows the Greek prenatal care schedule and paperwork (e-EFKA, gov.gr).

This week's building site

What the baby is building this week, what's done and what comes next, with a fruit-size comparison.

40 weeks at a glance

The whole journey in one timeline, by trimester.

Prenatal tests

The Greek prenatal test schedule, from the first scan to the CTG, with "add to Calendar".

e-EFKA & gov.gr paperwork

Checklists for maternity benefit, birth registration, the baby's AMKA and the birth allowance.

Kicks & contractions

Kick counter and contraction timer with a Lock Screen Live Activity, plus a widget.

Weight

A simple weight log with a chart.

Journal

Bump photos with a timelapse, letters to the baby sealed until a date, voice notes.

Us

Shared to-dos with your partner and optional sharing through iCloud.

Privacy, no asterisks

No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Your data stays on your iPhone. Only if you turn on partner sharing does a small set of data go through your own iCloud. Privacy policy

Medical note. Boumpouki provides general, informational content about pregnancy. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and does not replace your doctor or midwife. If you have any concern, talk to your doctor. In an emergency in Greece, call 166 or 112.
